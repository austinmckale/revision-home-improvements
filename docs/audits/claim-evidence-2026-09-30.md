# Operating and local claim evidence review

Reviewed on 2026-09-30. This review replaces unsupported assurances with useful scope-planning information. It is an evidence assessment, not owner testimony or an authentication of private business records. Changes are local and are not deployed.

## Evidence standard

- Repository text and a repeated claim on the live RHI Pros site show that the claim was published. They do not independently establish response capacity, equipment, training, completed work, customer identity, insurance coverage, or permitting experience.
- Supplied photos establish visible subjects. They do not establish a job address, construction responsibility, duration, concealed work, loss cause, insurance claim, or shared project sequence.
- Official municipal pages establish that permitting or historic review may be relevant. They do not establish RHI Pros' experience, trade authorization, or responsibility for a particular application.
- The existing service offering and six service-area hubs remain. No new municipality, route, service, customer, certification, or operational promise was added.

The repository search found marketing pages, form code, and photo assets rather than permit submissions, drying logs, equipment inventories, trade credentials, insurance certificates, or job contracts supporting the targeted operating claims. This is a limit of the available evidence, not proof that the business lacks those capabilities.

5. [Reading Self-Serve announcement](https://www.readingpa.gov/press-releases/1685-city-of-reading-introduces-online-permitting-and-licensing-portal-reading-self-serve): identifies construction permits, trade-related applications, and historic certificates in the municipal system. It does not identify RHI Pros as an approved permit applicant or trade contractor.

The replacement copy asks whether review applies and who is responsible; it does not promise approval or interpret a specific property's eligibility.

Used to keep reconstruction copy from implying specialist remediation competence. No medical advice, cleanup recipe, certification, or equipment promise was added.

## What can and cannot be concluded

These changes resolve the public-copy risk without requiring the owner to invent missing answers. They do not determine the true job address, contractual scope, customer, crew, credentials, insurer, or event sequence. Those facts are no longer presented as proof in the changed records. Existing slugs remain stable even where a slug contains an unverified town or loss label; current visible copy must supply the accurate evidence-limited presentation.

The broader root review handles photo-overview data, testimonials and review excerpts, credential/warranty pages, homepage and page-template labels, and complete-site validation. A municipal page and the presence of photos are not substitutes for business records. Private certificates, job files, and response logs were neither accessed nor fabricated.
